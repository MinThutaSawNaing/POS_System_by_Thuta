Online only. Financial data is never saved for offline use. The central warehouse owns supplier payables; shops own their sales and receivables.

Review before activating accounting

Activation starts today. Review existing product catalog and warehouse batch costs first: these become the opening FIFO costs. Zero-cost stock may prevent activation until corrected in the existing inventory workflows. This screen does not change those costs.

Actual opening balances

Enter actual cash, bank and other non-control balances for each location. Stock and existing customer receivables are brought in from the review above; do not enter them twice. Opening equity is computed from reviewed net assets, not entered manually.

Opening supplier balances (central warehouse)

Enter unpaid supplier balances with a supplier ID and reference. These create central payables, not new stock. Additional non-stock supplier bills can also be entered after activation.

All locations is for reports only. Choose a specific location before writing.
